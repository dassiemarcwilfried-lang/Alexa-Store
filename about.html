About Alexa Store

Welcome to Alexa Store

Alexa Store is an app-discovery platform designed to make exploring apps and games simple and convenient. Our goal is to organise useful app information in one place so visitors can discover software that matches their interests.

What you can find

  • Apps and games organised into categories.
  • Descriptions and key app information.
  • Links to app pages and official sources where available.
  • Featured or sponsored listings, clearly identified when present.

Our approach

We aim to make browsing straightforward, present information clearly and help visitors check the source and details of an app before installing it. App names, icons and trademarks may belong to their respective developers and owners.
